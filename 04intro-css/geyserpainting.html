Common Painting Terminology

Understanding industry terminology can help you make a highly informed decision for your next remodel:

Primer
A preparatory coating put on materials before painting to ensure better adhesion of the topcoat.
Matte Finish
A low-sheen paint texture that reflects minimal light, perfect for hiding minor surface imperfections.
Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs)
Solvents released into the air as paint dries. We actively prioritize low-VOC options for indoor safety.
