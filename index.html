Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship means using technology and the internet in a way that is safe, responsible, ethical, and effective.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law is the set of rules that governs what is legal and illegal in the online and digital world.

What is AUP?

An Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) is a set of rules that explains how people are allowed to use a computer network, device, or online service.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is when someone uses another person's words, ideas, or work and presents them as their own without giving credit.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is any form of cheating, lying, or unethical behavior in schoolwork or academic settings.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

AI-generated content rules are about being honest, following instructions, and using AI responsibly as a helper—not a shortcut for cheating.
