Who Were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence include

John Hancock
Known for his large, bold signature, which became synonymous with the word "signature" in American culture. He was the first person to sign the Declaration of Independence.
Thomas Jefferson
Wrote most of the Declaration of Independence and later became the third President of the United States.
Benjamin Franklin
One of the oldest signers at age 70. He was a famous inventor, diplomat, and political thinker.
John Adams
A leading advocate for independence who later became the second President of the United States.
Samuel Adams
A key figure in the movement for independence who helped organize the resistance to British rule.
Roger Sherman
The only person to sign the Continental Association, the Declaration of Independence, the Articles of Confederation, and the Constitution.
Charles Carroll
The wealthiest signer of the Declaration and the last surviving signer.
Richard Henry Lee
The Virginia statesman who introduced the resolution in Congress that led to American independence.

There were 56 signers in total, representing the 13 colonies. Among them, Jefferson, Franklin, Adams, and Hancock are generally the most widely recognized today because of their major roles in founding the United States. The signing itself was one of the defining moments leading to the American Revolution.
